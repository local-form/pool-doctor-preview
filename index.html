What does a new pool cost?

It depends on the size, the build type, the extras and your yard. Sketch yours in the planner above with the budget you have in mind, and Joe prices it with you.

Can you really tell what's wrong over the phone?

Often, yes. That's Joe's specialty: describe it (or text a photo) and he'll point you the right way, whether it's a quick fix you can do or a visit.

Do you clean pools?

No. The Pool Doctor builds, renovates and repairs. For weekly cleaning, ask Joe who he'd recommend.

Do you work with other contractors?
