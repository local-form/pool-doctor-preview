          11 — Questions

          Asked every season.

          When should I book an opening or a closing?

          Openings go fast from late April; book in March for May. Close after the water stays under about 65°F, usually mid-October in Essex County, before the first hard frost.

          My pool is green. Can it be saved without draining?

          Almost always. A green-to-clean visit shocks, brushes and filters it back over two to four days. Draining is a last resort and can damage a pool.

          Do care plans need a contract?

          In this example, no: plans run month to month and pause in winter. A real company sets its own terms.

          How long does a new gunite pool take?

          As an example, about six to ten weeks from dig to swim, depending on permits, weather and curing time. A real builder gives a dated schedule in the contract.

          Who handles permits?

          The builder files the plans and books the town’s inspections, including the steel inspection before concrete.

          Why add photos to a quote?

          A photo of the water and the equipment pad lets the technician bring the right parts the first time. In this example photos never leave your phone.
